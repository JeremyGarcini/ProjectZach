# Screen recording script — target 2:40

Record on an **iPhone 17 Pro** simulator, portrait, fresh launch.
Simulator → **File → Record Screen**. Keep the pointer deliberate; pauses read as
confidence.

Before recording: `⌘⇧H` to make sure nothing is left over, then **Start over**
(the ↺ button) so the scripted failure is armed.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 · The ask

Sit on the low-stakes screen without touching anything.

> "This is the moment Persona pauses. The largest thing on screen is the sentence
> that says what it wants to do — you can read it in half a second. Everything
> under it is why it thinks so, and it is deliberately quieter."

Point at: **Move dinner to 8:00 PM** → **7:30 → 8:00** → **Why now** → the
**Tonight** timeline with its sources.

## 0:20 – 0:30 · The pinned header and the progressive blur

Scroll up slowly, then back down.

> "The header and the switcher stay put. Content dissolves into the page as it
> passes underneath — a real progressive blur, built by stacking materials rather
> than with the private variable-blur API."

**Show this slowly.** It is the detail most likely to be missed at speed.

## 0:30 – 0:55 · Edit, then decline

1. Tap **Edit** (pencil).
2. Pick **8:30 PM**, tap **Save**.
3. Pause: the title *and* the button both now say 8:30 — the digits roll, they
   do not swap.
4. Tap **Not now** (✕).
5. Pause on **Dinner stays at 7:30 PM**.
6. Tap **Ask me again**.

> "Editing never approves. Saving puts you back on the request. And declining is
> a preference, not an error — nothing was called, nothing was changed."

## 0:55 – 1:25 · Approve, cancel, approve, undo

1. Tap **Move to 8:30 PM**.
2. Pause on **Calling Bar Pitti** — point out that the mark itself is the
   activity indicator, and that **Cancel** is right there.
3. Tap **Cancel**. Back on the untouched request.
4. Tap **Move to 8:30 PM** again and let it finish.
5. Pause on the success state and the undo line.
6. Tap **Undo**, pause on **Back to 7:30 PM**, then **Back to the request**.

> "Cancel is a real cancelled Task, not the interface pretending. And the bar at
> the bottom never moves — deciding, cancelling and recovering all happen in the
> same place."

## 1:25 – 1:50 · High stakes

1. Tap **High stakes**.
2. Pause.

> "Same page, same surfaces, same bar. What changes is that this leaves Persona
> and lands in someone's hand — so the draft is shown as the message he will
> actually receive, and there is a consequence spelled out that the low-stakes
> version does not have."

Point at: the **To** row → the **blue bubble** → **Commits you to another year**
→ the caution row. Scroll to show the blur again.

3. Tap **Edit** (pencil), change a word, **Save** — back on the request, nothing
   sent. Close.

## 1:50 – 2:20 · Review, hold, fail, retry

1. Tap **Review message**.

> "Approving does not send. It opens the last look — and the gesture changes."

2. **Press and hold** the button. Let it fill and send.

> "A tap is one accident away. A held press is not, and letting go at any point
> costs nothing. That is where the friction belongs."

3. It **fails**. Pause here.

> "The first send fails on purpose, so this state is real rather than
> theoretical. It names what did not happen — Daniel heard nothing — and says
> plainly that trying again is safe."

4. Tap **Try again**.

## 2:20 – 2:40 · Sent, undone, close

1. Pause on **Sent to Daniel** and the undo window.
2. Tap **Undo** → **Message unsent**.

> "Two stakes, one screen, one design system. Liquid Glass on the controls, never
> on the content. Colour only ever means the outcome — it never means the risk,
> because nobody should be able to approve on colour alone."
